PACKAGE 9 - PART 3

This part adds certificate generation and QR verification.

Dependencies required in backend package.json:

npm install pdfkit qrcode

Add to backend/app.js:

app.use(
    "/api/certificate-pdf",
    require("./routes/certificatePdfRoutes")
);

app.use(
    "/api/certificate-qr",
    require("./routes/certificateQrRoutes")
);

PDF generation endpoint:

POST /api/certificate-pdf/:id/generate-pdf

QR endpoint:

GET /api/certificate-qr/:id/qr

The generated QR points to the certificate verification page and
contains the certificate's unique verification code.

Run database/seed_notification.sql after Package 8 notification
tables are available.

Important:
The PDF generator uses the certificate record as its source.
A student name is read from certificate.student_name when that field
is joined/populated by a later student-profile integration. Until
then, the generated PDF safely falls back to "Student".

For production, replace the simple HTML/QR presentation with the
approved institutional certificate design and official branding.
