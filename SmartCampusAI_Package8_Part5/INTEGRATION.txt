PACKAGE 8 - PART 5

Add to backend/app.js:

app.use(
    "/api/notification-scheduler",
    require("./routes/notificationSchedulerRoutes")
);

app.use(
    "/api/automatic-alerts",
    require("./routes/automaticAlertRoutes")
);

Start the scheduler once after the Express app is initialized:

const notificationScheduler =
    require("./services/notificationSchedulerService");

notificationScheduler.start(30000);

The scheduler checks for notifications that have not yet been
processed and dispatches them according to user preferences.

Automatic alert endpoints:

POST /api/automatic-alerts/attendance
POST /api/automatic-alerts/fees
POST /api/automatic-alerts/academic
POST /api/automatic-alerts/placement

These endpoints are protected by role authorization.

IMPORTANT:
Do not expose these endpoints directly to untrusted public clients.
They are intended for internal modules such as attendance, fees,
academic results and placement modules.

Email and push providers remain adapters from Part 4. If they are not
configured, the notification remains safely recorded in-app while
external delivery is reported as failed/skipped by the provider layer.
