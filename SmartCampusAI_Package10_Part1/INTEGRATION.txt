PACKAGE 10 - PART 1

Package 10 begins the Placement & Career Management module.

Run database/schema.sql.

Add to backend/app.js:

app.use("/api/placement-profile",require("./routes/placementProfileRoutes"));
app.use("/api/placement-skills",require("./routes/placementSkillRoutes"));

Included:
- Student placement profile
- Career goal
- Preferred role/location
- LinkedIn/GitHub/portfolio links
- Placement status
- Student skill management
- Skill proficiency levels
- Verification flag foundation
- Student placement profile UI

Later Package 10 parts will add applications, company drives,
interviews, resume management, placement analytics and admin workflows.
